# Publishing on AWS Builder Center

## Why every team publishes

Builder Center articles are how the chapter and AWS see what teams built.
It's also required for hackathon submission — no article, no score for
that checklist item.

## Steps to publish

1. Create an account at builder.aws.com with the same email you use for AWS.
2. Start a new article and use the chapter template at
   /templates/aws-builder-center-article.md.
3. Lead with 3–5 screenshots of the working app before any code snippets.
4. Write one short paragraph on what you built and who it helps.
5. Add a section titled "Deploying this on AWS" that maps each part of your
   local prototype to a managed AWS service.
6. Tag the article with #aws-student-builders-groups #buildonaws
   #amazon-bedrock #rag.
7. Submit the article link in your hackathon submission form.

## What makes a strong article

Screenshots first, short paragraphs, and one clear "how it maps to AWS"
section. Judges skim articles — dense code dumps score lower than a few
well-captioned screenshots and a clear architecture explanation.

## Common mistakes

Publishing after the deadline, forgetting the required tags, and skipping
the AWS deployment section are the three most common point losses.
