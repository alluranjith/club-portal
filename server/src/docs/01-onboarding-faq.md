# New Member Onboarding FAQ

## Welcome to AWS Student Builder Groups

This chapter runs weekly build sessions, monthly workshops, and a yearly
campus hackathon. Membership is open to any student who wants to learn
cloud and AI by building real projects.

## How do I join a project team?

Post an intro in the club channel with your track (frontend, backend, AI/ML,
or cloud infra) and your available hours. Chapter leads pair new members
with an active team within one week.

## When is the next workshop?

Workshops run on the first and third Thursday of every month, 6:00–7:30 PM,
in the CS department seminar hall. The schedule is posted in
06-workshop-index.md and pinned in the club channel.
